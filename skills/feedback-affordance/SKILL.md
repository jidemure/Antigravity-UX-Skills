# Feedback and Affordance

A control's appearance, label, and response should agree. Provide immediate acknowledgement after an action; for slow operations show progress and prevent accidental duplicate work. Keep feedback near the affected control or content. Confirm high-impact irreversible actions with a clear description; prefer undo when safe and practical. Explain unavailable actions and how to enable them. Make hover behavior supplementary, never the only signal; support keyboard focus, touch, and screen readers. Do not use motion as the sole signal, and honor reduced-motion preferences. Use live announcements selectively so updates do not overwhelm assistive technology.
